ICCRE 2027 @ Hong Kong
Welcome to the official website of
2027 12th International Conference on Control and Robotics Engineering (ICCRE)
. The conference will be held in
Hong Kong during May 7-9, 2027
. The aim as well as objective of ICCRE 2027 is to present the latest research and results of scientists related to Control and Robotics Engineering topics.
The conference venue is Hong Kong Productivity Council (HKPC).
Robotics engineering
is an exciting field that is rapidly growing in popularity. With a background in mechanical, electrical, or software engineering, individuals can unleash their creativity and innovation in this discipline. Whether it's coming up with new uses for robots, designing improved systems, or maintaining industrial robots, there is a wide range of hands-on technical jobs available. Additionally, those looking for more experimental roles will find plenty of opportunities to explore and push the boundaries of robotics engineering.
We are thrilled to announce that the ICCRE conference in Hong Kong! Building upon the success of previous years, we are confident that this year's event will be even more remarkable and rewarding. On behalf of the organizing committee, we extend a heartfelt invitation to you. Come join us at ICCRE 2027 and be a part of the excitement and innovation that awaits in beautiful Hong Kong!

About Hong Kong
Hong Kong is a thrilling East-meets-West city where tradition meets modernity. Ride the Peak Tram for harbour views, take the cable car to the Tian Tan Buddha, and sail the historic Star Ferry across Victoria Harbour. At 8pm, the Symphony of Lights dazzles the skyline. Food lovers enjoy dim sum, egg tarts, milk tea, and wok-fried street snacks. Shop luxury or bargain at night markets. Yet three-quarters of Hong Kong is countryside 鈥?hike trails, discover ancient rocks in the UNESCO Geopark, or relax on beaches, all minutes from the city. This vibrant city will enrich your life.
