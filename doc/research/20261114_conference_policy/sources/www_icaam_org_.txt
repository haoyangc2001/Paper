Welcome to AAME 2027 @ Ningbo University, China
"Future Aerospace, Intelligent Manufacturing, and Autonomous Control"
The Conference Committee takes the pleasure to invite you to the
2027 the 10th International Conference on Aeronautical, Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering (AAME 2027)
to be held in
Ningbo, Zhejiang, China during March 26-28, 2027.
The AAME 2027 has inherited from the tradition of nine previous AAME conferences promoting an innovative environment for researchers to present their new research and engineering achievements and share their experience with researchers working in similar fields. AAME 2027 will create a global platform for researchers, scientists, academicians, policymakers, industry experts to share experiences, discuss research findings and acquire the desired knowledge in the subject from around the world with many networking opportunities. As the world is becoming more flexible and sophisticated, predictive solutions for the existing problems and future challenges in the field of Aeronautical, Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering need to be focused.
第十届航空航天与机械工程国际会议
AAME 2027将于2027年3月26-28日在浙江宁波隆重举行。本次会议聚焦航空航天与机械工程领域的前沿进展，特别围绕智能飞行器设计、先进推进系统、航空复合材料与轻量化结构、无人系统与自主控制、增材制造与精密加工、航空航天人工智能应用、可持续航空能源与动力、空气动力学与流体力学等热门方向展开深入讨论。会议旨在为全球航空航天与机械工程领域的专家学者、科研人员及产业界精英搭建一个高水平的国际合作交流平台，分享最新研究成果，共同探讨当前面临的挑战与未来技术突破方向。诚邀海内外学者踊跃投稿并莅临参会，共襄学术盛会！
