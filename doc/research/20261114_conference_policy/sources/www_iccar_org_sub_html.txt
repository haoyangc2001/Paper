Paper Submission 投稿指南
投稿规则 / Rules for Submission
1
Full paper submissions should be limited to 4-10 pages, (
Word Template
,
LaTeX Template
), including the references and any possible appendices. Submissions longer than 10 pages will be rejected without review.
全文投稿篇幅限制为4-10页（
Word 模板
、
LaTeX 模板
），包含参考文献及可能的附录。超过 10 页的投稿将不经过审稿直接退回。
2
All submissions will be blind reviewed by the Program Committee on the basis of technical quality, relevance to scope of the conference, originality, significance, and clarity.
所有投稿将由程序委员会根据技术质量、与会议主题相关性、原创性、重要性和清晰度等方面进行盲审。
3
Please note that full-paper registration covers the publication of accepted papers of up to 6 pages. Any pages exceeding this limit will be subject to an additional page charge of 70 USD / 510 RMB per excess page (510 RMB).
请注意，全文注册将包含文章出版6页。超出页数（超过 6 ）收取每页70美金/510元的超页费。
4
At least one author of each accepted paper is required to register to the conference and present the paper. Only registered and presented papers will be published in the conference proceedings.
每篇录用论文至少需有一位作者注册参会并作报告。仅注册并报告的论文将被收录于会议论文集中。

摘要投稿 / Abstracts
如果您不打算发表全文，仅希望展示您的研究成果，我们接受以摘要形式提交报告申请。
Abstracts are accepted for presentation proposal, if you don't want to publish the full paper, just present your work, you can submit an abstract.
会议论文集 / Conference Proceedings
After a careful reviewing process, all the accepted and presented full papers will be published by ICCAR Conference Proceedings. At least one author is required to register and present at ICCAR.
经过严格的审稿流程后，所有录用并注册报告的全文论文将由
ICCAR Conference Proceedings
出版。
审稿流程 / Reviewing Procedure
01
The paper selection will follow a
double-blind review
process, meaning that both the authors and reviewers will remain anonymous during the paper selection process.
论文遴选遵循
双盲审稿
流程，即在整个论文遴选过程中，作者与审稿人均保持匿名。
02
Submitted manuscripts will conduct an initial screening by plagiarism checker.
投稿稿件将通过
查重系统
进行初步筛查。
03
Submitted manuscripts are reviewed by an ICCAR Scientific Technical Committee that covers the topic of the manuscript.
投稿稿件由覆盖稿件主题领域的 ICCAR
科学技术委员会
进行评审。
04
Submissions are rated by the committee members based on (i) originality (ii) design novelty (iii) advancement within the field and (iv) technical quality. Authors should provide clear evidence of what is novel in their work and/or the extent to which it advances the state-of-the-art. Many submissions will be stronger in novelty or advancement, so both are equally weighted by the committee. A high level of technical quality and clarity is a requirement for all submissions
委员会将基于以下标准对稿件进行评分：(i)
原创性
(ii)
设计新颖性
(iii)
领域进展
(iv)
技术质量
。作者应清晰说明其工作的创新之处及对领域前沿的推进程度。
05
Submitted manuscripts must not have been previously published or currently being considered by others for publication.
投稿稿件
不得
此前已发表或正在被其他出版机构考虑录用。
学术不端声明 / Plagiarism Policy
ICCAR 对学术不端行为采取零容忍态度
ICCAR is utterly intolerant of plagiarism. Submitted papers are expected to contain original work executed by the authors with adequate, proper and scholarly citations to the work of others. It is the job of the authors to clearly identify both their own contribution(s) and also published results / techniques on which they depend or build. Reviewers are charged to ensure these standards are met.
ICCAR 对抄袭行为持零容忍态度。投稿论文应包含作者原创工作，并对他人工作给予充分、适当且学术规范的引用。作者有责任清楚标识其自身贡献及所依赖或基于的已发表结果/技术。审稿人需确保这些标准得到满足。
If the author is found to commit an act of plagiarism, the following acts of sanction will be taken:
若发现作者存在抄袭行为，将采取以下制裁措施：
Reject the article submitted or delete the article from the final publications.
拒绝所投稿件或从最终出版论文集中删除该文章。
Report the authors violation to his/her supervisor(s) and affiliated institution(s).
向作者导师及所属机构通报其违规行为。
Report the authors violation to the appropriate overseeing office of academic ethics and research funding agency.
向相关学术道德监督办公室及研究资助机构通报作者违规行为。
Reserve the right to publish the authors name(s), the title of the article, the name(s) of the affiliated institution and the details of misconduct, etc. of the plagiarist”.
保留公布抄袭者姓名、文章标题、所属机构名称及不当行为详情等信息的权利。
