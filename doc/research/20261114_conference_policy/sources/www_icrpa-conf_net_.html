Welcome to Join in ICRPA 2027

2027 International Conference on Robot Perception and Automation (ICRPA 2027), as the workshop of RICAS 2027, sponsored by Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Hubei University of Arts and Science, will be held from March 19-21, 2027 in Chongqing.

ICRPA is a premier academic event that will bring together leading researchers, engineers, and industry professionals from around the world to share cutting-edge advancements and innovative solutions in Robot Perception and Automation. Scheduled to take place in Chongqing, this conference will serve as an important platform for presenting high-quality research results, discussing emerging trends, and fostering collaborations that address current challenges and future directions in these rapidly evolving fields. Through keynote speeches, technical sessions, and networking opportunities, ICRPA 2027 aims to facilitate meaningful exchanges of ideas and promote technological breakthroughs that will shape the next generation of smart systems and intelligent applications. We cordially invite scholars and practitioners to join us in this exciting endeavor to advance knowledge and drive innovation across these interconnected disciplines.

Accepted papers, after proper registration and presentation, will be included in the Conference Proceedings, which will be published published by IOP Publishing in the Journal of Physics: Conference Series and indexed by Ei Compendex, Scopus, CPCI and other important databases.

Submission and Review Process

Submissions should be sent to our submission system before the submission deadline. All submissions will be subjected to preliminary review and peer-review (double blind).

All papers that conform to the submission guidelines will be sent to two review steps. The preliminary review will take 1-3 working days by the conference committee members, if the submission passed the preliminary review, then it will be sent for peer-reviewed(double blind) by 2-3 Committee Members. The review result comes out within 20-30 days and will be sent to the authors by email along with the review comments.

If the paper needs necessary revising (minor or major revisions), the revised version should be submitted again for peer review by the initial reviewer(s). Authors are allowed to submit the revisions within 10 days. If the revisions are accepted by the initial reviewer(s), then the revised papers will be accepted by the conference ICRPA 2027 do not accept multiple submissions, plagiarism, and self-plagiarism. Submissions in violation will be rejected without review
