ICCAR 2027 Call for Papers

2027 13th International Conference on Control, Automation and Robotics (ICCAR)

Following the success of previous editions, 2027 13th International Conference on Control, Automation and Robotics (ICCAR) will be held at Nanchang, Jiangxi, China during April 23-25, 2027.

ICCAR 2027 is sponsored by East China Jiaotong University, supported by Nagoya Institute of Technology, Japan, Osaka Metropolitan University, Japan, Dalian Maritime University, China, Laurentian University, Canada, Sungkyunkwan University, Republic of Korea, etc.

ICCAR wishes to encourage academic excellence for young people in this field. Therefore, the organizing committee initiated several awards to raise the importance of academic achievement in our future professionals. Know more about the prize.

ICCAR 2027 WIE Best Paper Awards

The ICCAR 2027 WIE Best Paper Awards recognise women’s research contributions in control, automation and robotics, and encourage participation in Women in Engineering (WIE) activities. Two categories are offered: the WIE Best Student Paper Award and the WIE Best Early-Career Researcher Paper Award.

Prize: One winner in each category will receive a certificate and USD 100.
WIE Best Student Paper Award — USD 100
WIE Best Early-Career Researcher Paper Award — USD 100

Award Rules: ICCAR 2027 WIE Best Paper Awards (PDF)
