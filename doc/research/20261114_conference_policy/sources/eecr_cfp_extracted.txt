2027 13th International Conference on Electrical Engineering, 
Control and Robotics (EECR) will be held in Shenzhen, China 
during April 23-25, 2027. The conference is sponsored by Harbin 
Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. We welcome contributions 
that promote the exchange of ideas and rational discourse 
between educators and researchers from around the world, and 
we look forward to your participation in EECR 2027!
Accepted papers will be included in EECR 2027 Conference 
Proceedings, which will be indexed by EI Compendex, Scopus, and 
other indexing services.
