Welcome Message

We are pleased to announce and cordially invite you to 2027 the 2nd International Conference on Mechanical, Electronic and Robotics Engineering (MERE 2027), which will be held in Wuhan, China from May 21-23, 2027.

MERE 2027 serves as a high-level international platform designed to bring together global researchers, engineers, and industry representatives. The conference is dedicated to fostering frontier technological innovation and promoting cross-disciplinary integration within the fields of mechanical, electronic, and robotics engineering.

Wuhan Polytechnic University will serve as the host, with China University of Geosciences (Wuhan) as the co-host. This edition is organized by the School of Mechanical Engineering and the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Wuhan Polytechnic University. We warmly welcome experts, scholars, and technical professionals from around the world to participate in this event to share your latest research findings and engage in profound academic exchange.

我们诚挚地邀请您参加即将在2027年5月21日至23日于中国武汉举行的第二届机械、电子与机器人工程国际会议 (MERE 2027)。 MERE 2027 旨在打造一个高水平的国际交流平台，汇聚全球研究人员、工程师和行业代表。会议致力于推动机械、电子和机器人工程领域的前沿技术创新，促进跨学科融合与发展。

本次会议由武汉轻工大学主办，中国地质大学（武汉）联合主办，并由武汉轻工大学机械工程学院和电气与电子工程学院联合承办。 我们热忱欢迎世界各地的专家、学者和技术人员莅临本次盛会，分享最新研究成果，开展深入的学术交流。

Authors - Full Paper Submission / 作者全文投稿

[Definition] The "Author" category is specifically for participants who submit full-length manuscripts for peer review and formal publication.针对提交全文稿件、参加同行评审并寻求正式出版的参会人员。

[Submission Standards] Submissions must be original, unpublished, and written in English. All papers will undergo a plagiarism check (Similarity index below 20%).稿件须为原创且未经发表的英文论文。所有投稿均需经过学术查重（重复率须低于 20%）。

[Paper Length] A full paper submission should typically range from 4 to 6 pages, including all figures, tables, and references.全文投稿通常建议长度为 4 至 6 页，包含所有图表及参考文献。

[Presentation & Publication] Accepted authors are required to present their work (Oral or Poster) at the conference to ensure the paper's inclusion in the proceedings.录用作者须在会议上进行学术报告（口头或海报展示），以确保论文收录于论文集。

Presenters - Abstract Submission / 报告人 - 摘要投稿

[Definition] The "Presenter" category is designed for participants who wish to deliver an oral or poster presentation to share their latest research or work-in-progress, without publishing a full paper in the proceedings.适用于希望通过口头报告或海报展示分享最新研究成果或在研项目，但**不需要**在会议论文集中正式出版全文的参会人员。

[Submission Standards] A descriptive abstract (typically 200-400 words) should be submitted for review. 需提交一份描述性摘要（通常为 200-400 字）。

[Presentation & Certificate] Accepted presenters will be assigned a 15-minute oral presentation slot or a poster display space. An official presentation certificate will be issued on-site.录用的报告人将被安排 15 分钟的口头报告或海报展示位。组委会将在现场颁发正式的参会报告证书。

[Note on Publication] Please note that abstract-only submissions will be included in the conference program for communication purposes but will NOT be sent for indexing in digital libraries.请注意，仅摘要投稿的内容将收录于会议程序册供交流参考，但**不会**提交至数字图书馆检索或出版。

Listener Registration / 听众注册

[Definition] The "Listener" category is for participants who wish to attend the conference without submitting a paper or making a presentation.适用于希望参加会议但**不提交论文且不做报告**的参会人员。

[Benefits] Listeners have full access to all keynote speeches, technical sessions, and workshops. 听众可以全程参与所有主旨演讲、技术分会场及工作坊。

How to Register / 注册流程:
  1. Online Registration: Complete your registration via the Online Registration System.
    在线支付：通过在线支付系统完成缴费。
  2. Confirmation: Email your Payment ID and Registration Form to mere_conf@chairmen.org to confirm your attendance.
    邮件确认：将您的支付 ID 及注册表发送至邮箱以确认参会。
