About ICCRT
2027 5th
International Conference on Control and Robot Technology (ICCRT)
will take place in
Urumqi, China, from June 11 to 13,
2027
. Sponsored by the Xinjiang Mechanical Engineering Society,
the conference provides an international forum for researchers,
engineers, academics, and industry professionals to present
their latest research, exchange ideas, and discuss emerging
challenges in control, robotics, and related technologies.
The conference will cover a broad range of
topics, including control theory and applications, robotics and
autonomous systems, intelligent control, automation, machine
learning for robotics, and emerging technologies. It aims to
promote the exchange of knowledge and experience, foster
interdisciplinary collaboration, and explore new opportunities
for research and development. ICCRT also welcomes young
researchers to engage with the international research community
and contribute to advances in control and robot technology.
Conference Publication
All submissions will undergo a blind
peer-review process based on technical quality, relevance to the
conference topics, originality, significance, and clarity.
Accepted papers that are presented at the
conference and completed the registration process will be
included in the conference proceedings, which will be submitted
for consideration for inclusion in major academic databases.
