Notes:
All papers must be presented by one of the
authors. At least one author must register for the
conference and pay the fee prior to Regular Registration
deadline in order for the paper to be published in the
conference proceedings and accepted in the final
program.
One regular registration can cover a paper within 14
pages, including all figures, tables, and references for
one main author of the paper. More than 14 pages, you
need to pay 30 per page.
One regular registration covers only one author.
Co-authors SHOULD also register in-order to join in the
conference and display their name in the conference
materials.
One day tour is not included in the regular
registration.
All the registration fees quoted above are inclusive the
Taxes.
If you would like to register as committee member,

icccv@umails.com
to apply.

Registration Regulations
1. Student discount is only available if the first author of the paper is a student. And please provide valid student proof.
2. If the length of the full paper exceeds 14 pages, the cost of each extra page is
30 USD / 200 RMB / page.
3. Each paid registration covers only one paper; Additional paper price is only applicable to the same first author who already has a regular registration.
4. Onsite registration is applicable to presentation only and Delegates. If you
would like to register onsite, you are required to give us a notice before March
15th, 2027 for preparing you materials.
Refund Policy
Cancellation of paid registration is possible under the terms
listed below:
* 20 days ahead of the conference: 30 USD cancellation fee.
* 10-20 days ahead of the conference: cancellation fee of 20% of the
registration fee.
* Within 10 days ahead of the conference: no refund.
* Non-refund on delay or force majeure events including, without limitation,
natural disasters, fires, earthquake or storm, strikes, failures of public
utilities or common carriers, acts of war, or intervention, acts restraints or
regulations of any governmental authority, including compliance with any order
of any governmental considerations.
Note: Cancellation and refund request must be made formally by email to
conference secretary at
icccv@umails.com
.
