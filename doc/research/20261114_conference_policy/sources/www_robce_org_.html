2027 7th International Conference on Robotics and Control Engineering (RobCE 2027)

May 20-23, 2027 / Shenzhen, China

2027 7th International Conference on Robotics and Control Engineering

2027 7th International Conference on Robotics and Control Engineering (RobCE 2027) will take place in Shenzhen, China during May 20-23, 2027, which is organized by Southern University of Science and Technology and Southeast University, supported by The Hong Kong Polytechnic University - Nanjing Technology and Innovation Research Institute, Hong Kong Society of Mechanical Engineers (HKSME) and Undertaken by Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Fully Actuated System Control Theory and Technology. Focusing on the international research and key application domains of robot and control engineering, around the theme of "AI-Driven Intelligent Control and Next-Generation Robotic Systems", RobCE 2027 is going to conduct high level academic communication and display the latest achievements, build the international collaborative innovation platform.

We warmly welcome prospective authors to submit your research papers to RobCE 2027, and share your latest research results and valuable experiences with other top-scientists, engineers and scholars from all over the world.

Call for Papers-Topics

RobCE 2027 is scheduled to include high-quality paper presentation sessions revealing the latest research findings, and to engage participants in interesting discussion sessions. The RobCE organizing committee invites researchers worldwide to submit papers and share the valuable experiences with the scientists from the world.
Topics of interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

Track I: Robot Perception, Navigation and Control
Track II: Embodied Intelligence and Robot Learning
Track III: Autonomous Robot Systems
Track IV: Industrial Robot Control and Application
Track V: Human-Robot Interaction Control
Track VI: Robot Control Theory and Methods
Track VII: Novel Robot Design and Analysis
Track VIII: Rehabilitation and Medical Robot Technology
Track IX: AI-Driven Robot Systems
Track X: Robot Industrialization and Application
Track XI: Tele-robot and Its Applications

Peer Review Policy

All submissions to the RobCE will be peer reviewed, and each paper will be reviewed by 2-3 independent reviewers. Reviewers evaluate submissions based on the requirements of the conference proceedings, predefined criteria, and quality, completeness, originality and grammar of the research presented. The notification of acceptance or rejections will be sent to authors when the peer review is done. Manuscript requirements:
1. The manuscript must be written in English;
2. The manuscript should be written in accordance with the standard of template;
3. The topics of manuscript should be relevant to conference topics;
4. The manuscript should be no less than 8 pages (Single column) or 5 pages (Double columns);
5. Plagiarism and duplicate submission are prohibited;
6. Innovation and scientific value are essential.

⚠️ Important Note about ACM's New Open Access Publishing Model:
ACM has introduced a new open access publishing model for the International Conference Proceedings Series (ICPS). Authors based at institutions that are not yet part of the ACM Open program and do not qualify for a full geographic waiver will be required to pay an article processing charge (APC) to publish their ICPS article in the ACM Digital Library.
