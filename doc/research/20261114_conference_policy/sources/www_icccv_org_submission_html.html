Submission

Submit Now !



1. Submission Requirements

1). Language
English is the official language of the conference; the paper should be written and presented only in English.

2). Template Download
Paper Template (Word)

3). Submission Types
* Abstract submission for presentation only without publication.
* Full paper submission for both presentation and publication.

4). Paper Length
Each full paper should be no less than 8 pages, including all figures, tables, and reference. When it exceeds 14 pages, extra page will be charged at 30 USD / 200 RMB / Page. 


2. Submission Process

1). Download the Paper Template (Word)  for formatting your paper.
2). Submit your paper to Online Submission System or icccv@umails.com.
- via online submission system: an account is needed, if you don't have, please register first.
- via mailbox: the email subject should be named as "Submission-ICCCV 2027-Full Paper/ Abstract".
3). The conference secretary (icccv@umails.com) will contact you within three working days once receiving your submission.
