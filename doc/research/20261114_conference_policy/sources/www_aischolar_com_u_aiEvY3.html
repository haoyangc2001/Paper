About the Conference

The 2026 International Symposium on Robotics, Autonomous Systems and Control (ISRASC 2026) will be held from December 18 to 20, 2026 in Guangzhou, China. Focusing on "Robotics", "Autonomous Systems" and "Control", the conference provides an international platform for experts, professors, scholars, and engineers from universities, research institutes, enterprises, and other institutions to share professional experiences, expand professional networks, exchange new ideas face-to-face, and present research findings. The symposium aims to explore the major challenges and research directions in these fields, promote the development and application of related theories and technologies in academia and industry, establish commercial or research connections, and seek global partners for future endeavors.
