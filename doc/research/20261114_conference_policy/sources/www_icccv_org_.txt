Tianjin,
China

April 2-4, 2027
Theme: Advances in Computer
Vision, Optical Imaging and Intelligent Control
The 9th International Conference on
Control and Computer Vision (ICCCV 2027) will be held in
Tianjin, China, during April 2–4, 2027, and is organized by
Tianjin University of Technology and Education.
ICCCV 2027 brings together researchers, academics, engineers,
and industry professionals from around the world to explore the
latest advances in computer vision, optical imaging, intelligent
sensing, artificial intelligence, and intelligent control. Under
the theme “Advances in Computer Vision, Optical Imaging and
Intelligent Control,” the conference highlights emerging
research in computational imaging, 3D vision, hyperspectral and
multispectral imaging, optical sensing, intelligent machine
vision, robotics, and autonomous systems.
The conference provides a multidisciplinary platform connecting
the communities of optics, imaging, photonics, computer vision,
AI, and intelligent systems, fostering the exchange of
innovative ideas, research findings, and practical solutions.
ICCCV 2027 welcomes researchers and professionals to Tianjin to
share their work, explore emerging technologies, and build new
international collaborations in next-generation vision and
imaging.
