SCIEI

Welcome to Science and Engineering Institute

The Science and Engineering Institute (SCIEI) is a non-profit academic association dedicated to promoting scientific and engineering research and education, to providing a platform for researchers, educators, and industry professionals to connect and collaborate on cutting-edge research and development projects. SCIEI organizes conferences, workshops, and training programs with universities, scientific institutions, non-profit bodies, to promote knowledge sharing and professional development, to provide opportunities for participants to present their research, network with peers, and learn about the latest advancements in their fields, to converge, exchange insights, and propel the field forward.

SCIEI has cooperated and is now collaborating with lots of well-known academic organizations and universities. Such as University of Aveiro, University "Federico II" of Napoli, Capitol Technology University, Orleans University, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Washington University in St. Louis, University of Huddersfield, National Defense Academy of Japan, Polytechnic University of Porto, York University, Istanbul Technical University, Beihang University, Tokyo University of Science, Nagoya Institute of Technology, etc.

Our Missionn

SCIEI’s Mission is to promote and enhance the dialogue in education among the institutions devoted to Science and Engineering fields through:
-Promotion of best practice standards in the service of international education.
-Promote and enhance the dialogue in education among the institutions devoted to Science and Engineering fields through: -The facilitation of relevant forums, training and information exchange.
-Creation and dissemination of knowledge.
- Production of publications used as a database document for research works, projects and innovation activities held on the international education field.
SCIEI believes that this is best achieved through international cooperation and promotes the development of closer links among relevant institutions and individuals around the world. SCIEI supports that such international cooperation can help countries learn from each other and promotes the dissemination of scientific and engineering activities.
SCIEI intends to achieve the mentioned objectives and get an international visibility by the organization of international conferences and by interacting with public and private organisms from all parts of the world.
