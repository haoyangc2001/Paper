Peer Review Policy
All submissions to the RobCE will be peer reviewed, and each paper will be reviewed by 2-3 independent reviewers. Reviewers evaluate submissions based on the requirements of the conference proceedings, predefined criteria, and quality, completeness, originality and grammar of the research presented. The notification of acceptance or rejections will be sent to authors when the peer review is done.
Manuscript requirements:
1. The manuscript must be written in English;
2. The manuscript should be written in accordance with the standard of template;
3. The topics of manuscript should be relevant to conference topics;
4. The manuscript should be no less than 8 pages (Single column) or 5 pages (Double columns);
5. Plagiarism and duplicate submission are prohibited;
6. Innovation and scientific value are essential.
