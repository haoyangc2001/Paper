Call for Papers Submission

Welcome to AIACT 2027

Welcome to the official website of 2027 11th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Control Technologies (AIACT 2027). AIACT 2027 whose theme this year is "Intelligent Autonomy & Integrated Control – AI-Driven Innovation for Industrial and Cross-Domain Automation", is organized by The Hong Kong Polytechnic University-Nanjing Technology and Innovation Research Institute, co-organized by Southeast University and Hong Kong Society of Mechanical Engineers (HKSME) with media support from Sensors (MDPI). It will be held in Nanjing, China from February 26 to 28, 2027. AIACT 2027 is a not-to-be-missed opportunity that distills the most current knowledge on a rapidly advancing discipline in one conference. Join key researchers and established professionals in the field of Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Control Technologies as they assess the current state-of-the-art and roadmap crucial areas for future research.

Peer-review Statement

All submissions to the AIACT 2027 will be sent to 2-3 independent reviewers for peer review. Manuscript requirements:
1.The manuscript must be written in English;
2.The manuscript should be written in accordance with the standard of template;
3.The topics of manuscript should be relevant to conference topics;
4.Plagiarism and duplicate submission are prohibited; 
5.Innovation and scientific value are a must.

Publication & Indexing

* AIACT 2027

1) All the registered and presented papers will be included in the volume of ACM International Conference Proceedings Series (online publishing), the publisher will submit articles to Engineering Village, Scopus, Web of Science and other databases for review and indexing after publication.

2) Selected papers(accepted and presented) with high quality and great extension, which are presented on conference, will be recommended to publish in:

A. Modelling (ISSN: 2673-3951), indexed by ESCI, Scopus and other databases.

B. Sensors (ISSN: 1424-8220)

C. Automation (ISSN: 2673-4052), indexed by ESCI, Scopus, and other databases.



Papers published in the conference proceedings in the past years were indexed by Ei Compendex, Scopus and other databases. More details can be found at: AIACT History

Call for Participants

 

Join as Presenter

If you are interested in giving presentation on conference, without publishing your paper in the proceeding, you can choose to attend AIACT 2027 as a Presenter. As presenter, you need to submit the abstract and title of your presentation before register. For further information, please contact us at: aiact@vip.163.com

 

Join as Listener

AIACT 2027 is an unmissable conference. It is a good chance and an effective plateform to meet many renowned experts and reseachers in the field of Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Control Technologies. You are welcome to attend this great event. You need to complete the registration as Listener before the registration dealdine.
