Dear Colleagues and Distinguished Guests,
On behalf of the entire Organizing Committee, it is my distinct honor and pleasure to extend a warm welcome to all participants of the
2027 12
th
International Conference on Automation, Control and Robotics Engineering (CACRE 2027)
. We are delighted to host this prestigious event in the historic and vibrant city of Beijing, China, from August 12 to 15, 2027.
For over a decade, since its inception in 2016, the CACRE conference series has served as a premier international forum, bringing together leading researchers, engineers, and students from across the globe. It is a platform where innovative ideas are exchanged, cutting-edge research is showcased, and lasting collaborations are forged.
This year, we are proud to announce that CACRE 2027 is organized by
the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University
and co-organized by
the Hong Kong Society of Mechanical Engineers (HKSME)
, technically sponsored by
IEEE RAS
, with support from the
Shenyang Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences
,
Tsinghua University Press
,
Xi'an Jiaotong University
,
Dalian Maritime University
and
HKUST-Wuxi Joint Laboratory for Robotic Embodied Dexterity and Intelligence
. Embracing the theme
“Robotics Science and Intelligent Systems,”
our program is thoughtfully curated to reflect the most exciting advancements and future challenges in automation, control, and robotics. We have assembled an outstanding lineup of plenary and invited speakers, and we look forward to the stimulating discussions that will arise from the technical sessions and networking events.
I would like to express my deepest gratitude to all our authors, reviewers, session chairs, sponsors, and dedicated staff. Your hard work and commitment are the bedrock of this conference’s success and reputation.
To all our attendees, whether you are presenting your latest findings, seeking new knowledge, or building professional networks, I hope you will find this conference intellectually rewarding and personally enriching. Beijing, with its unique blend of ancient heritage and modern dynamism, is the perfect setting for our scientific exploration and cultural exchange.
I wish you all a memorable and productive conference.
Welcome to Beijing, and welcome to CACRE 2027!

Peer Review Instructions
All submissions will be reviewed and each paper will be reviewed by 2-3 independent reviewers. If the paper needs revising, it should be resubmitted for peer review again. Manuscript requirements:
1. Manuscripts must be written in English;
2. The manuscript should be formatted in accordance with the template;
3. The topics of paper should be relevant to conference topics;
4. Plagiarism is prohibited;
5. Innovation and scientific value is a must.

Call for Participants
Join in as Presenters
CACRE 2027 is now accepting oral presentation and poster presentation. If you are interested in presenting your research on the conference, without publishing your paper in the proceeding, please submit the abstract to us.
Join in as Listeners
If you are interested in attending the conference to participate in this gathering in the field of Conference on Automation, Control and Robotics Engineering, you are welcome to join us and share your ideas!
Join in as Reviewers
Reviewers evaluate article submissions to CACRE, based on the requirements of the conference proceedings, predefined criteria, and quality, completeness and accuracy of the research presented. They provide feedback on the article and the research, suggest improvements and make a recommendation to the editor about whether to accept, reject or request changes to the article.
