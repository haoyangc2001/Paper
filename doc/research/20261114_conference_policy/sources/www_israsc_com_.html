About ISRASC 2026

2026 International Symposium on Robotics, Autonomous Systems, and Control

The 2026 International Symposium on Robotics, Autonomous Systems and Control (ISRASC 2026) will be held from December 18 to 20, 2026 in Guangzhou, China. Focusing on "Robotics", "Autonomous Systems" and "Control", the conference provides an international platform for experts, professors, scholars, and engineers from universities, research institutes, enterprises, and other institutions to share professional experiences, expand professional networks, exchange new ideas face-to-face, and present research findings. The symposium aims to explore the major challenges and research directions in these fields, promote the development and application of related theories and technologies in academia and industry, establish commercial or research connections, and seek global partners for future endeavors. The conference facilitates the exchange of the latest global trends in scientific and technological research within relevant fields, links top, active, and cutting-edge academic resources in key areas both domestically and internationally, and drives the transformation of research achievements as well as the aggregation of talent, technology, and capital through experience sharing and intellectual, thereby enhancing new drivers of development.

Publication

All papers, both invited and contributed, will be reviewed by two or three expert reviewers from the committees. After a careful reviewing process, all the accepted papers of ISRACS 2026 will be published , and will be submitted to EI Compendex and Scopus for indexing.

Note: All submitted articles should report original research results, experimental or theoretical, not previously published or under consideration for publication elsewhere. Articles submitted to the conference should meet these criteria. We firmly believe that ethical conduct is the most essential virtue of academics. Hence, any act of plagiarism or other misconduct is totally unacceptable and cannot be tolerated.
