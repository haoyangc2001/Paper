Welcome Message

Dear Researchers, Scientists, Engineers, Academics, and Graduate Students,

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to Hong Kong, China, for 2027 12th Asia-Pacific Conference on Intelligent Robotic Systems (ACIRS), to be held from July 23–25, 2027.

ACIRS 2027 provides an international forum for researchers, engineers, developers, and practitioners from academia and industry to share the latest advances in intelligent robotic systems, exchange ideas, and explore emerging research directions and opportunities.

We warmly invite you to present your latest research, innovative ideas, and practical experiences, and to engage in meaningful discussions with colleagues from around the world. We also welcome suggestions for topics, activities, and scientific programs that can further enrich the conference.

Your participation will help foster knowledge exchange, new collaborations, and continued advances in intelligent robotic systems. We are excited to welcome you to Hong Kong, China, and to share an inspiring and productive ACIRS 2027 with you.

Warmest regards,

Welcome to ACIRS 2027

Intelligent robotic systems have been rapidly advancing and finding widespread applications in recent years. This interdisciplinary field encompasses a broad range of research areas, including artificial intelligence, control theory, computer vision, and mechanical engineering. Intelligent robotic systems are increasingly being deployed across diverse industries, such as manufacturing, healthcare, transportation, and entertainment, where they are expected to perform increasingly complex tasks with greater autonomy, flexibility, and adaptability. Achieving these capabilities requires advances in intelligent perception, decision-making, motion and control, as well as the seamless integration of sensors, actuators, and other robotic components.

2027 12th Asia-Pacific Conference on Intelligent Robotic Systems (ACIRS) will be held in Hong Kong between July 23-25, 2027. The conference is sponsored by City University of Hong Kong, China. It's a great opportunity to get together and share ideas and knowledge among the Industry experts, researchers and academics. Several current and upcoming frontier technologies, innovative solutions, research results, as well as enterprises related to intelligent robotic systems and their applications will be presented.

2027 第十二届亚太智能机器人系统国际会议 (ACIRS) 将于2027年7月23-25日在香港举办。该会议旨在为智能机器人系统等领域的专家学者建立一个广泛有效的学术交流平台，以便让参会人员及时了解行业发展动态、掌握最新技术，快速、准确地解决当前面临的难题。大会将安排主旨报告，特邀报告，以及数个专题讨论会（形式包括口头报告，海报报告，视频报告等）。在此，我们诚挚邀请您的参加，希望能与您在香港相会！

The conference will feature distinguished keynote speakers, invited talks, technical sessions, and specialized tracks addressing a broad range of topics in intelligent robotic systems and their applications. We warmly invite researchers and practitioners to submit their latest work and contribute to the technical program. Contributions addressing artificial intelligence and related technologies with potential applications in intelligent robotics are also welcome. We also welcome suggestions for topics, activities, and scientific sessions that could further enrich the conference program. We look forward to welcoming you to Hong Kong and to making ACIRS 2027 a productive and engaging event for the global robotics community.

Call for Papers

To promote academic excellence and foster the exchange of innovative research, we cordially invite scholars, experts, professionals, and students from universities, research institutions, and industry to submit their original high-quality papers.

Interest for submission include, but are not limited to:

Track 1: Robot Design, Mechanics, and Mechatronics
Track 2: Robotic Perception, Sensing, and State Estimation
Track 3: Artificial Intelligence, Robot Learning, and Embodied Intelligence
Track 4: Robot Motion Planning, Control, and Manipulation
Track 5: Autonomous and Multi-Robot Systems
Track 6: Human–Robot Interaction and Collaborative Robotics
Track 7: Robotic Systems Engineering, Simulation, and Integration
Track 8: Robotics Applications and Emerging Robotic Technologies

ACIRS 2027 Awards

Best Paper Award | 最佳论文奖
One outstanding student paper will be selected by the Conference Committee based on innovation, originality, impact, writing quality, and presentation.

Best Oral Presentation Award | 最佳口头报告奖
One outstanding oral presentation from each technical session will be selected by the Session Chair based on originality, technical merit, applicability, and presentation quality.

Best Poster Award | 最佳海报报告奖
All the accepted and registered poster papers. It will be selected by every poster session chairs.
