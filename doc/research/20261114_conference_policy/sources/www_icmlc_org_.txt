Welcome to ICMLC 2027
AI Converges in Hetao, Innovation Beyond Boundaries: Frontiers in Machine Learning and Global Collaboration
AI聚河套 · 智创无界：机器学习前沿与全球协同创新
We are pleased to welcome you to
2027 19th International Conference on Machine Learning and Computing, which will be held in Shenzhen, China during February 26-March 01, 2027
. The International Conference on Machine Learning and Computing (ICMLC) is grand annual conference on machine learning algorithms, computational statistics, mathematical optimization, computer engineering, computer science and other related subject since 2009. It will provide opportunities for researchers in this field to share their ideas and reinforce collaboration. As such, the conference has achieved a remarkable number participants while also enable profuse exchanges among academic and industrial researchers over the years.
ICMLC 2027 is co-sponsored by
The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen
and
Shenzhen Loop Area Institute
, and supported by Southwest Jiaotong University, Southeast University, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Sun Yat-sen University, South China Agricultural University, Sichuan University, South China University of Technology and so on.
2027年第19届机器学习与计算国际会议(ICMLC 2027)定于2027年2月26日-3月1日在中国深圳举办。会议旨在为机器学习与计算研究领域的专家学者提供一个交流相关领域最新研究成果的平台。会议征稿主题包括自适应系统、神经网络与支持向量机、商业智能、混合动力和非线性系统、生物统计学等研究领域。
2027年第19届机器学习与计算国际会议(ICMLC 2027)将由香港中文大学（深圳）和深圳河套学院联合主办，并得到西南交通大学，东南大学，南京理工大学，中国科学院深圳先进研究院，中山大学，华南农业大学，四川大学，华南理工大学，美国丹佛都会州立大学等高校的技术支持。
Publication | 会议出版
All peer-reviewed and presented papers from ICMLC 2027 will be published as post-proceedings in a volume of the Springer book series: Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems. The book series is now indexed by Ei Compendex, Scopus, INSPEC, etc. All books published in the series are submitted for consideration in Web of Science.
After reviewing, accepted abstracts will be invited to make the presentation only.
