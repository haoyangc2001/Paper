Paper submission

 

 

Submission Methods | 投稿方式

  • English is the official language of the conference.
    Full paper should be at least full 4 pages and at most 10 pages under the format.
    Regular registration fee only covers one 6 pages paper. Addtional pages will cost extra fees.
    Prospective authors are invited to submit full (and original research) papers in electronic (DOC or PDF) format through the following link:

    http://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=icccr2027

    The author is suggested to use the ICCCR Formatting Template.

     Full Paper-Doc. Version         Full Paper-LaTex Version         Abstract Template
Important Dates | 重要时间
  • Abstract & Full Paper Submission Deadline: January 1st, 2027
    投稿截止时间：     2027年1月1日
  • Notification Day: February 1st, 2027
  • 审稿结果通知时间：2027年2月1日
  • Registration Deadline: February 20th, 2027
  • 注册截止时间： 2027年2月20日
To Participant the Conference

As Author, you can publish the paper in conference proceedings and deliver a presentation on ICCCR 2027. You need to submit Full-length Manuscript for review.

As Presenter, you can deliver a presentation on ICCCR 2027, orally or poster presentation. The presented manuscript WILL NOT be published. Abstract is necessary to submit.

As Delegate, you can attend all the presentation sessions.
Policy on Multiple Submissions
  • ICCCR will not accept any paper which, at the time of submission, is under review, is accepted for publication, or has already been published in another conference or a journal. Authors are also expected not to submit their papers elsewhere during the ICCCR 2027 reviewing period.
  • ICCCR is utterly intolerant of plagiarism. Submitted papers are expected to contain original work executed by the authors with adequate, proper and scholarly citations to the work of others. It is the job of the authors to clearly identify both their own contribution(s) and also published results / techniques on which they depend or build. Reviewers are charged to ensure these standards are met.
  • If the author is found to commit an act of plagiarism, the following acts of sanction will be taken:
    1. Reject the article submitted or delete the article from the final publications.
    2. Report the authors violation to his/her supervisor(s) and affiliated institution(s)
    3. Report the authors violation to the appropriate overseeing office of academic ethics and research funding agency.
    4. Reserve the right to publish the authors name(s), the title of the article, the name(s) of the affiliated institution and the details of misconduct, etc. of the plagiarist”
