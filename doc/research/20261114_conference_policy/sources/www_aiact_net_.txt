Welcome to AIACT 2027
Welcome to the official website of 2027 11
th
International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Control Technologies (AIACT 2027). AIACT 2027 whose theme this year is
"Intelligent Autonomy & Integrated Control – AI-Driven Innovation for Industrial and Cross-Domain Automation"
, is organized by
The Hong Kong Polytechnic University-Nanjing Technology and Innovation Research Institute
, co-organized by
Southeast University
and
Hong Kong Society of Mechanical Engineers (HKSME)
with media support from
Sensors (MDPI)
. It will be held in
Nanjing, China
from
February 26 to 28, 2027
. AIACT 2027 is a not-to-be-missed opportunity that distills the most current knowledge on a rapidly advancing discipline in one conference. Join key researchers and established professionals in the field of
Artificial Intelligence, Automation and Control Technologies
as they assess the current state-of-the-art and roadmap crucial areas for future research.
Peer-review Statement
All submissions to the AIACT 2027 will be sent to 2-3 independent reviewers for peer review.
Manuscript requirements:
1.The manuscript must be written in English;
2.The manuscript should be written in accordance with the standard of template;
3.The topics of manuscript should be relevant to conference topics;
4.Plagiarism and duplicate submission are prohibited;
5.Innovation and scientific value are a must.
