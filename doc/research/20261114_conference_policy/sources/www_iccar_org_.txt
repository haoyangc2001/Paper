Following the success of previous editions,
2027 13th International Conference on Control, Automation and Robotics (ICCAR)
will be held at
Nanchang, Jiangxi, China during April 23-25, 2027
.
ICCAR 2027 is sponsored by
East China Jiaotong University
, supported by Nagoya Institute of Technology, Japan, Osaka Metropolitan University, Japan, Dalian Maritime University, China, Laurentian University, Canada, Sungkyunkwan University, Republic of Korea, etc.

the prize

ICCAR 2027 WIE Best Paper Awards
The ICCAR 2027 WIE Best Paper Awards recognise women’s research contributions in control, automation and robotics, and encourage participation in Women in Engineering (WIE) activities. Two categories are offered: the WIE Best Student Paper Award and the WIE Best Early-Career Researcher Paper Award.
Prize:
One winner in each category will receive a certificate and
USD 100
.

USD 100
