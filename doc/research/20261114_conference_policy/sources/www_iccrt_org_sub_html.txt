Submission Guideline
Full Paper Submission
Prospective authors who would like to publish the paper are invited to submit original, unpublished work. Please follow the instructions below carefully.
Full papers must be prepared using the conference paper format template.
Papers should have no less than 4 pages (including title, authors and affiliations, figures, and references).
Abstract Submission
Prospective authors who don't have publication intention are invited to submit a digest prepared according to the layout requirements provided in the template.
The Abstract must not exceed 200 words.
