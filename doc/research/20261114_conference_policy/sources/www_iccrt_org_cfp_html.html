2027 5th International Conference on Control and Robot Technology  |  Urumqi, China

Call for Papers

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following areas:

Track 1Control Theory, Systems Modeling and Optimization

  • Linear and Nonlinear Control
  • Adaptive, Robust and Optimal Control
  • Model Predictive and Intelligent Control
  • System Modeling, Identification and Simulation
  • System Dynamics, Stability and Estimation
  • Optimization Algorithms and Decision-Making
  • Data-Driven and Learning-Based Control
  • Fault Detection, Diagnosis and Fault-Tolerant Control

Track 2Robotics and Autonomous Systems

  • Robot Design, Development and Control
  • Robot Kinematics, Dynamics and Motion Planning
  • Mobile Robots and Autonomous Navigation
  • Industrial, Service and Field Robotics
  • Aerial, Underwater and Legged Robots
  • Humanoid, Soft and Bio-inspired Robotics
  • Multi-Robot Systems and Cooperative Robotics
  • Teleoperation and Human-Robot Interaction

Track 3Automation, Mechatronics and Intelligent Manufacturing

  • Industrial Automation and Process Control
  • Mechatronic Systems and Applications
  • Intelligent Manufacturing and Production Systems
  • Industrial Robotics and Automated Assembly
  • Intelligent Equipment and Machinery
  • Motion Control and Servo Systems
  • Embedded and Real-Time Control Systems
  • Monitoring, Maintenance and Industrial Diagnostics

Track 4Artificial Intelligence, Perception and Intelligent Systems

  • Artificial Intelligence for Control and Robotics
  • Machine Learning and Reinforcement Learning
  • Computer Vision and Image Processing
  • Intelligent Sensing and Sensor Fusion
  • Pattern Recognition and State Estimation
  • Intelligent Planning and Autonomous Decision-Making
  • Cognitive Systems and Intelligent Agents
  • AI-Enabled Modeling, Prediction and Optimization

Track 5Networked Control, Distributed Systems and Cyber-Physical Systems

  • Networked and Distributed Control
  • Multi-Agent Systems and Cooperative Control
  • Cyber-Physical Systems
  • Industrial Internet of Things
  • Networked Sensing and Communication
  • Cloud and Edge Computing for Control Systems
  • Distributed Automation and Intelligent Networks
  • Network Robotics and Remote Control

Track 6Control and Robotics Applications and Emerging Technologies

  • Control Applications in Transportation and Autonomous Vehicles
  • Robotics and Automation in Agriculture
  • Control and Automation in Energy and Environmental Systems
  • Intelligent Systems for Mining and Industrial Operations
  • Medical, Rehabilitation and Assistive Technologies
  • Control and Robotics in Challenging Environments
  • Emerging Robotic Platforms and Intelligent Equipment
  • Novel Applications of Control, Automation and Robotics
