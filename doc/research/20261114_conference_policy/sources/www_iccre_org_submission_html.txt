Article acceptance requirements
The criteria for an article to be accepted for publication include:
1. The article should be original writing that enhances the existing body of knowledge in the given subject area. Original review articles and surveys are acceptable, even if new data/concepts are not presented.
2. Results reported have not been submitted or published elsewhere (although expanded versions of conference publications are eligible for submission).
3. Experiments, statistics, and other analyses are performed to a high technical standard and are described in sufficient detail.
4. Conclusions are presented in an appropriate fashion and are supported by the data.
5. The article is presented in an intelligible fashion and is written in Standard English.
6. Appropriate references to related prior published works must be included.
Reviewing Process
1. All manuscripts submitted for publication will undergo a rigorous blind peer-review process. It is imperative that all submissions meet the scientific criteria of novelty and academic excellence.
2. The corresponding author is entrusted with the crucial task of ensuring that the article's publication has received the approval of all other co-authors. In addition, they bear the responsibility for the paper throughout the submission and peer review process.
3. Review comments will be provided to you for necessary revisions. It is required that you make the necessary revisions and submit the revised paper on or before the prescribed day.
