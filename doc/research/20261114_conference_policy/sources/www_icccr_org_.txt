Welcome to ICCCR 2027!
2027 7th International Conference on Computer, Control and Robotics (ICCCR-www.icccr.org) will be held during the period May 14-16, 2027 in Chengdu, China. The conference is sponsored by Southwest Jiaotong University.
With the rapid advancements in computer science, control technologies, and robotics, these fields have achieved significant breakthroughs, driven by developments in artificial intelligence, machine learning, quantum computing, embedded systems, and sensor technologies. However, several challenges persist, such as increasing computational complexity demanding more efficient hardware and energy consumption, the need for enhanced security and robustness in distributed control systems, and the limitations of robotics in unstructured environments regarding adaptability and autonomy. ICCCR 2027 aims to provide a premier academic platform for global experts, scholars, and researchers to exchange knowledge, share cutting-edge findings, and explore solutions to these pressing challenges while envisioning future directions for the field.
The conference will feature keynote speeches, invited talks, and specialized sessions covering a wide range of topics, including multimodal integration, intelligent decision-making and human-machine interaction, control optimization for complex systems, and security in distributed systems. Presentations will take various forms, including oral presentations, poster sessions, video demonstrations, and virtual presentations, fostering diverse and interactive exchanges. We cordially invite you to participate in ICCCR 2027 to contribute to advancing academic innovation and industrial progress. We look forward to welcoming you to the beautiful city of Chengdu in May 2027!
2027 第七届计算机、控制和机器人学国际会议（ICCCR 2027）将于2027年5月14-16日在中国·成都举办，由西南交通大学主办。
随着计算机科学、控制技术与机器人领域的快速发展，这些学科在人工智能、机器学习、量子计算、嵌入式系统和传感技术的推动下取得了诸多突破。然而，行业仍面临诸多挑战，包括计算复杂度增加对硬件和能耗的高要求、分布式控制系统的安全性和鲁棒性问题，以及机器人在非结构化环境中的适应性和自主性限制。ICCCR 2027 旨在为全球专家、学者和研究人员提供一个广泛有效的学术交流平台，共同探讨这些技术的最新进展与应用，聚焦解决当前技术瓶颈，并探索未来发展方向。
会议将通过主旨报告、特邀报告、专题讨论会等形式呈现学术前沿与实践热点，交流内容涵盖计算机科学、控制技术与机器人学的多模态融合、智能决策与人机交互、复杂系统控制优化、分布式系统安全等议题。报告形式多样，包括口头报告、海报报告、视频报告及在线报告，为与会者提供多元化的互动机会。在此，我们诚挚邀请您的参与，共同推动学术创新与行业进步，期待在美丽的成都与您相聚！
